APP NAME: TOONMAKER STUDIO
PACKAGE ID: com.toonmakerstudio.app
SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80): Make cartoon scenes with characters, voices, sounds and video.
FULL DESCRIPTION:
TOONMAKER STUDIO lets you create your own cartoon scenes on your phone.
- 6 characters: boy, girl, two friends, a cat and a dog. Drag them anywhere.
- Actions: stand, walk, run, sit, sleep, chase.
- Expressions: happy, sad, cry, angry, sulk. Change hair style and colour.
- Backgrounds: paper, park, room, night, or your own photo.
- Voice: type text and the characters speak. Write dialogues for two or more characters.
- Sound effects: meow, woof, bed creak, cloth rustle, footsteps.
- Record your scene as a video and share it.
- Your project is saved automatically.
CATEGORY: Art & Design (or Entertainment)
TARGET AUDIENCE: General audience / all ages (NOT designed only for children)
CONTENT RATING: answer the questionnaire honestly: no violence, no user-generated sharing inside the app, no ads (for now).
